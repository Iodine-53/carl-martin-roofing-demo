3. What does it cost — and what moves the number?

Honest 2026 Tampa ranges, not "call for a quote." The range is the starting point; the free inspection pins the exact number.

$8,500–$15,000 for most Tampa homes in 2026. What moves the number: roof size and pitch, shingle vs. metal, soft decking that needs replacing, and accessibility. A written quote should list these as line items — ours does. One $900 repair today is a $14,000 replacement after the next tropical storm — and insurers are already non-renewing roofs over 15 years old.

$350–$900 covers most repairs — a cracked pipe boot, blown-off shingles, a valley reseal. We fix what's failing and tell you plainly what can wait a year.

1–3 days for most Tampa homes: tear-off to final inspection, with a magnet sweep of the yard. You're only home for the loud part of day one.

If more than 25% of a roof section is damaged within 12 months, Florida code can require the whole section to meet current code — for older roofs. Roofs built or replaced after March 2009 to the 2007 code (or newer) may qualify for partial repair, if the repaired part meets today's code. We tell you which side of that line your roof is on before we quote — not after the inspector does.
